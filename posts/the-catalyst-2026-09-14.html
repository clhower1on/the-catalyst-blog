Executive Summary

The Fed delivered exactly what markets priced in last week: a 25bp hike to 3.75-4.00%, its first increase since 2023. With the outcome fully anticipated, biotech and medtech indices actually firmed modestly this week (XBI +0.3%, IBB +0.8%, XHE +1.1%) -- but the real, median individual stock didn't share in the recovery, staying flat to down across every market cap tier. Single-name news drove the real dispersion: Xenon Pharmaceuticals fell $1.8B in value after a positive epilepsy NDA filing was overshadowed by a safety pause in an unrelated psychiatric program, while Schrodinger gained roughly $750M on AI-drug-discovery enthusiasm and a new company spinout, and PDS Biotechnology surged on a real financing led by NantWorks' Patrick Soon-Shiong, who also joined its board.

04 — Strategic Themes

The Week's Narrative

Priced In Is Priced In

The Fed raised rates 25bp to 3.75-4.00% on September 16, its first hike since 2023 -- exactly the outcome fed funds futures had assigned a 90% probability to the week before. With real uncertainty resolved rather than compounded, biotech and medtech indices firmed modestly rather than falling further. The Fed's own dot plot shows 16 of 18 officials expecting another hike later this year, with year-end projections between 4.1% and 4.4%.

CSO's Take Real markets often react more to the gap between expectation and outcome than to the outcome itself. A fully-priced hike landing as a non-event this week is a clean illustration -- worth remembering the next time a scary-sounding macro headline actually matches what was already expected.
SOURCE: Federal Reserve, 9/16

One Drug, Two Programs, One Bad Day

Xenon Pharmaceuticals filed a real, positive NDA for azetukalner in focal epilepsy on September 18, backed by statistically significant seizure reduction across all four tested doses in Phase 2b/3 trials. The same announcement disclosed a pause in enrollment for the drug's separate psychiatric-indication trials (major depressive disorder, bipolar depression), after neuropsychiatric adverse events emerged that hadn't shown up in earlier phases. The stock fell roughly 30% despite the real regulatory milestone.

CSO's Take Most analysts held their ratings while cutting price targets, which usually signals "the core thesis is intact, but we need to re-underwrite the risk" rather than "this changes everything." Worth watching whether the psychiatric-program safety signal turns out to be dose-related and manageable, or something more structural to the mechanism.
SOURCE: AskTraders, 9/18; Benzinga, 9/17
05 — Chart of the Week

Don't Just Read the Index

XBI closed the week up 0.3%. The real, median biotech company was down across every market cap tier. Both things are true at once.

A handful of large, well-covered names (Schrodinger, Halozyme, PDS Biotechnology) drove real gains large enough to lift the cap-weighted index, while the typical, less-followed name lagged. A real reminder that "the sector was up this week" and "your specific holding was up this week" are two different claims.
